Rumble Drop

Stack the falling blocks, fill rows to blow them up.

  • Tap to turn right
    Three taps turn left
  • Drag left or right to move
  • Drag down slowly to drop faster
  • Flick down to slam
  • Flick up to hold a piece

Keyboard: ←/→ move, ↓ drops faster, ↑/Z turn right/left, Space slams, C holds, P pauses.
